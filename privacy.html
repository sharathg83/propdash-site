Privacy policy

propdash is a personal tool used only by its owner. It has no other users.

What it accesses

Using Google's gmail.readonly permission, propdash reads messages in the owner's own Gmail account. It cannot send, delete or change email.

What it does with it

Messages and attachments are copied to a private server controlled by the owner, where fixed rules pick out utility bills, payments and property-manager statements for the owner's records. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Revoking access

The owner can withdraw access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
